# 谱里 Brand Reference

## Overview

谱里 is a warm, approachable digital family-tree product positioned as “年轻人的第一份家谱”. Its visual language pairs a paper-like cream canvas with a deep botanical green and a confident red mark. The experience should feel personal and archival without becoming old-fashioned: generous whitespace, rounded UI surfaces, delicate relationship lines, and clear Chinese typography.

## Colors

- **Paper**: `#F7F3E8` — primary background
- **Parchment**: `#EEE7D6` — cards and soft panels
- **Forest**: `#173F38` — primary text, lines, and navigation
- **Forest Soft**: `#2D5C52` — secondary UI and relationship nodes
- **Red Seal**: `#A64632` — logo, CTA, emotional emphasis
- **Slate**: `#65736D` — supporting copy
- **Ink**: `#23352F` — high-contrast text

## Typography

- **Chinese display**: use a system serif stack (`Songti SC`, `STSong`, `Noto Serif CJK SC`) for the emotional headline.
- **Chinese UI**: use a system sans stack (`PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`) for labels and product UI.
- **Hierarchy**: large serif statements, compact sans labels, high readability at mobile/social scale.

## Elevation

Use flat paper surfaces with thin forest borders and restrained shadows. Cards are gently rounded, with depth coming from layered parchment panels and offset relationship lines rather than glossy gradients or heavy drop shadows.

## Components

- **Red Seal Mark**: rounded square logo with white “谱” character.
- **Family Constellation**: connected portrait/name nodes arranged as a living tree.
- **Archive Cards**: cream cards containing names, dates, and short memory fragments.
- **Invitation CTA**: rounded red button that turns a private archive into a shared family action.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the paper background warm and calm.
- Use forest green for structure and relationship lines.
- Let the red mark punctuate key moments and calls to action.
- Keep motion gentle, organic, and legible.

### Don'ts

- Do not use neon colors, metallic UI, or generic blue SaaS gradients.
- Do not make the family tree look like a sterile org chart.
- Do not overload the frame with tiny labels or dense data.
- Do not use dark full-screen backgrounds that erase the archival warmth.

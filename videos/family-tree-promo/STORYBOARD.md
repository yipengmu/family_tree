# Storyboard — 谱里 20s product promo

**Format:** 1920×1080
**Audio:** silent kinetic typography with soft visual rhythm; music-ready negative space
**VO direction:** if recorded later, warm, intimate, unhurried Chinese delivery
**Style basis:** DESIGN.md and live site identity

## Global direction

Paper-white canvas, forest-green structure, red seal accents. The promo moves from a single memory fragment into a living connected family archive. Use only a few words per beat, large enough to read without audio.

## Beat 1 — Memory, 0–4s

Warm paper background. A small red seal mark drifts in from the left while a handwritten-feeling memory card appears: “外婆 / 1958”. The line is intimate and quiet. Headline: “有些故事，值得被留下。” Use slow card rise and a soft paper grain made from low-opacity dots.

## Beat 2 — See the family, 4–9s

The memory card expands into a constellation of connected names. Green relationship lines draw on. Nodes gently float at different depths. Headline: “看家谱，名字不再只是名字。” The product promise is made visual: names become relationships.

## Beat 3 — Continue the story, 9–15s

Transition by following a relationship line into three archive cards: “一张老照片”, “一段家常话”, “一个还没写完的故事”. Cards stack with slight offsets. Red accent dots travel along the green lines. Headline: “续家谱，把记忆一代代接上。”

## Beat 4 — Product close, 15–20s

Constellation, cards, and lines settle into a calm family tree diagram behind the actual red logo asset. Large lockup: “谱里”. Subline: “年轻人的第一份家谱”. CTA pill: “看家谱 · 续家谱 · 管家谱”. End with a small red pulse at the center of the tree.
